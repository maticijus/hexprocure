# Market research, 2026-10-20

Collected by a research agent with web search on 2026-10-20 for `pricing-tiers.md`
and `erp-integration-route.md`. Most share and pricing figures come from third-party
aggregators, not vendors or analysts. No source gave a reliable SME-segment market
share, so the ERP ranking is by regional dominance. Gaps are marked "not found".

Regional leaders outside the table: Fortnox (SE, 80 % or more of firms with five or
more staff), Visma e-conomic (DK), Cegid, Sage and Pennylane (FR), Exact and AFAS (NL).
Not covered with verified figures: FreeAgent, Zoho Books, Sage 50. Czech market: no
direct source; Pohoda assumed leader, Money S3 and KROS the rivals.

Vendors with official MCP servers: Xero, QuickBooks, NetSuite, Business Central.
Odoo has community projects only.

Research agent's recommended integration priority for an EU SME product:
1. Odoo (widest EU SME spread, open API, easiest to build against)
2. Business Central (DACH, Nordics, mid-market)
3. Xero and QuickBooks as one OAuth2 accounting adapter
4. DATEV (essential for Germany, gated behind a partner programme)
5. Pohoda and Money for CZ and SK, Exact for Benelux
6. NetSuite, then SAP

Recommendation: expose HexProcure's own MCP server rather than building per-ERP MCP
clients; generic CSV, UBL and Peppol export covers the long tail.
